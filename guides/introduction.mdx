TapMind connects your app to premium ad demand through your existing mediation stack. You add TapMind as a Mediation Adapter inside the mediation platform you already use - AdMob, Google Ad Manager, AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay, or TopOn - and TapMind competes for your ad requests like any other network.

## Choose your SDK

Check with your TapMind account manager to confirm which SDK is suitable for your app.

[Mediation Adapters (GMA SDK)](/mediation-adapter-gma-sdk/android/admob) : 

A lightweight Mediation Adapter for an existing mediation stack. Android, iOS, and cross-platform frameworks including Flutter, React Native, Unity, Cocos, Cocos2dx, and Capacitor.

[Mediation Adapters (GMA Next-Gen SDK)](/mediation-adapter-gma-next-gen-sdk/android/admob) : 

Native Android (AdMob, Google Ad Manager, AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay), Flutter Android (AdMob), and Unity (AdMob, Unity LevelPlay) on the next-generation GMA API.

## How to use these docs

Open an engine in the top bar, then choose Mediation Adapters (GMA SDK) or Mediation Adapters (GMA Next-Gen SDK) in the sidebar, then the mediation guide. Each mediation has a Changelog under it.

Core SDK changelogs: [Android SDK (GMA)](/changelog/sdk/gma-mediation-android), [Android SDK (GMA Next-Gen)](/changelog/sdk/nextgen-gma-mediation-android), and [iOS SDK](/changelog/sdk/gma-mediation-ios).

Every page follows the same five steps:

1. Prerequisites
2. Install
3. Configure the mediation dashboard
4. Verify
5. Troubleshoot

Need help? Contact your TapMind account manager for the placement values and configuration specific to your account.
