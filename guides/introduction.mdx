TapMind connects your app to premium ad demand through your existing mediation stack. You add TapMind as a custom adapter inside the mediation platform you already use - AdMob, Google Ad Manager, AppLovin MAX, or Unity LevelPlay - and TapMind competes for your ad requests like any other network.

## Choose your SDK

Check with your TapMind account manager to confirm which SDK is suitable for your app.

- **[Custom Adapter (GMA SDK)](/custom-adapter-gma-sdk/android/admob)**: a lightweight adapter for an existing mediation stack. Android, iOS, and cross-platform frameworks including Flutter, React Native, Unity, Cocos, Cocos2dx, and Capacitor.
- **[Custom Adapter (GMA Next-Gen SDK)](/custom-adapter-gma-next-gen-sdk/android/admob)**: native Android (AdMob, Google Ad Manager, AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay), Flutter Android (AdMob), and Unity (AdMob, Unity LevelPlay) on the next-generation GMA API.
- **[Orchestration SDK](/orchestration-sdk/android)**: lightweight Android ad serving through placement codes, with no third-party mediation platform.

## How to use these docs

Open an engine in the top bar, then choose **GMA Adapter Mediation SDK** or **Next Gen Adapter Mediation SDK** in the sidebar, then the mediation guide. Each mediation has a **Changelog** under it.

Core SDK release notes are under **SDK changelog**: **GMA Mediation SDK Android**, **NextGEN GMA Mediation SDK Android**, and **GMA Mediation SDK iOS**.

Every page follows the same five steps:

1. Prerequisites
2. Install
3. Configure the mediation dashboard
4. Verify
5. Troubleshoot

**Need help?** Contact your TapMind account manager for the placement values and configuration specific to your account.
